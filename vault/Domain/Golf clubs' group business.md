# Golf clubs' group business

Researched 6 October 2026, after the user asked whether a golf club would ever want a group
booking feature, or whether that only makes sense for a hotel. A quick look at real club sites -
details vary by club and change, so the links are the source.

## It's a standard side business

Golf clubs commonly sell **company and group days** - corporate golf, society days, customer
events - alongside ordinary tee times. It isn't hotel-like; it's closer to a restaurant's
private-events page.

- **Packages by group size** - e.g. corporate packages for 8 and 20 participants with a PGA pro
  introduction (Mackmyra Golf); company golf for 24 or 48 people, or a full course booking for up
  to 90 (Sparren GK).
- **Price level** - a day with green fee, lunch and a prize ceremony runs roughly 800-2,500 kr
  per person.
- **Lead time** - UK clubs recommend enquiring 3-6 months ahead for group days, against days or
  weeks for an individual tee time.
- **Terms** - often a minimum of about 12 golfers, and a deposit to secure the date.
- **How it's booked today** - an enquiry form or a phone call, then a quote by hand.

## Clubs don't build booking themselves

Checked 9 October 2026. Swedish clubs book tee times through an external system - **Min Golf**,
the Swedish Golf Federation's, or **Sweetspot**, which also does dynamic green-fee pricing and
whose tee times are now bookable through Min Golf. The club's website links to or integrates
with it. Our site mocks such a system ([[011 The catalogue comes from a mocked golf booking system]]).

## What it means for us

That process - request, quote, deposit, confirmed date - is Proposales' shape. The feature should
be **"Company & group days"**, with a full-course booking as one option among the packages; "book
the whole venue" overstated it. See [[The app]].
