# The brief

This app is a technical case for a job application at Proposales. Summary of the email:

- Use the [Proposales](https://proposales.com) API to build anything - useful, silly or playful.
- **Bonus:** involve an LLM, or use the [Vercel AI SDK](https://ai-sdk.dev).
- **Host it on Vercel** (Hobby plan) and share the link, plus the GitHub repo.
- They care about **thought process over polish** - "not about what you build, it's about how
  you think and approach things".
- Due "Monday next week" - 2026-10-12 if the email arrived the week of 2026-10-05.

## How we pick what to build

- **Involve an LLM** - it's the stated bonus. The Vercel AI SDK fits naturally since it's
  hosted on Vercel.
- **No special access** - avoid ideas that depend on the Proposales connection powerup (see
  [[Proposales overview]]); it's outside our control before the deadline.
- **Not something Proposales already does** - check [[Proposales overview]] first. Rebuilding
  their dashboard or inbox widget would show we didn't look.
- **One clear, finished flow** beats several half-built ones.
- **Show the thinking** - record why we picked the idea, what we left out and what we'd do
  next in [[Decisions]]. That's what they're grading.
- **Integration basics** - key kept server-side, only the fields we need, sensible errors.

Candidates are in [[Proposales integration ideas]].
