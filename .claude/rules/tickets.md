## Writing tickets

- **The title says what changes**, for someone who has never seen the board: *"FE-3 The form
  asks how many you are before anything else"*, not *"FE-3 Headcount first"*. A long title is
  fine; a label only the people in the discussion understand is not.
- **The ticket is the work.** What it asks for and what it produces - a table, a checklist,
  the outcome - are written in the ticket itself, not in another document it points to.
- **Its state lives in the ticket:** status, *Blocked by*, *Done when* and what happened. A board
  only lists tickets under a column.
- **A ticket doesn't direct other tickets** ("update BE-2 from this"): a ticket that needs
  another's result says so in its own *Blocked by*.
